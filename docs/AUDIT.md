Rejected candidates, with reasons:

- **Replace the libwebm demuxer with AVFoundation.** AVFoundation declares no WebM/Matroska file type (`grep -rli "webm\|matroska" $SDK/System/Library/Frameworks/AVFoundation.framework/Headers/` → none); the wire contract is WebM and fixed. Raw packets over IPC would remove the demuxer but is a wire-contract change (out of scope; already the "Raw packets to iOS" Status row).
- **Replace `StreamDataSource` + `PipedInputStream` with a media3 source.** media3-datasource 1.11.1 ships no `InputStream` DataSource (class list from the AAR in `~/.gradle/caches`: Aes, Asset, ByteArray, Content, DataScheme, Default, DefaultHttp, File, FileDescriptor, Http, HttpEngine, Placeholder, Priority, RawResource, Resolving, Stats, Tee, Udp). `FileDescriptorDataSource` would need an OS pipe and closes its stream per load, which `StreamDataSource.kt:13-15` explains the loader does at the end of every load. 47 lines is the floor.
- **Drop `cpp/video/VP9HeaderParser.h` (143 lines) in favour of the container's vpcC.** The remuxed V_VP9 TrackEntry carries no CodecPrivate (hex probe: TrackNumber, UID, FlagLacing, Language, CodecID, TrackType, DefaultDuration, Video{512,288}, no `63A2`); the profile exists only in the frame header, and `WebmVP9Session.mm:11-19`'s hand-built vpcC is what VideoToolbox gets.
- **Replace the ring + demux thread with direct feeding from the IPC reader.** CLAUDE.md invariant: the playout margin lives in the ring as compressed bytes; moving it into bare-kit's IPC/worklet backpressure is unverified on device and is the class of change that produced the 2026-10-09 overflow. Round 4: "rewrite only with a reason".
- **Move to `AVSampleBufferVideoRenderer` to leave the iOS 18-deprecated layer members.** The members used (`enqueueSampleBuffer`, `flush`, `status`, `error`, `requiresFlushToResumeDecoding`, `isReadyForMoreMediaData`, `requestMediaDataWhenReadyOnQueue`, `stopRequestingMediaData`: `AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer.h:94,103,110,139,148,168,194,212,219`, `API_DEPRECATED(... ios(8.0, 18.0))`) are the only API below iOS 17: `sampleBufferRenderer` is `ios(17.0)` (`:303`) and the deployment target is 16.6, the consuming app's floor (decided 2026-10-12), so the migration stays out until that floor reaches 17; a dual path is more code, not less.
- **Flush-on-pull in `WebmVideoDecoder.feedLayer` (`ios/WebmVideoDecoder.mm:131-135`) as redundant with `WebmDisplayLayerWatch`.** It is a second recovery path in the frozen-picture area; the observed recovery (docs/DEVICE_VALIDATION.md 2026-10-09, 35.1 s) went through the notification, but removing the pull-path flush cannot be shown safe without eyes on the phone, which Status still lists as open.
- **Fold `ios/WebmEngineMetrics.{h,mm}` (49 lines) into the engine.** `WebmPlaybackEngine.mm` is at 400 lines, the warn line; the fold saves ~20 lines and spends budget.
- **Android `LoadControl` 30 ms buffers.** Android playback tuning is out of scope.
